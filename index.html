How Tideway balances a passage

Each route is split into open-water and protected segments. Protected segments remain at 10 knots or less. The planner finds a feasible open-water speed within the vessel’s 24-knot limit, then weighs fuel consumption, whale exposure and arrival freshness for the selected cargo.

Freshness responds to journey duration and reefer temperature. Fuel consumption scales with speed cubed over time. Save a passage to add it to your impact overview.
